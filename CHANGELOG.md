## [2.8.1] - 2026-10-25

### Fixed
- **Halloween Event Drop Pool & RNG Odds Harmonization** (`admin_modules/admin_economy.js`):
  - Added all 13 canonical Halloween rewards into the Mission Control probability manager (`FALLBACK_ODDS_DATABASE.case_hw`): 4 complete sets (`bundle_witch_full`, `bundle_vampire_full`, `bundle_ghost_full`, `bundle_demon_full`), 3 room locations (`hw_room_patch`, `hw_room_crypt`, `hw_room_mist`), and 6 individual wardrobe items (`hw_pumpkin_helm`, `hw_vampire_cape`, `hw_witch_hat`, `hw_skeleton_suit`, `hw_bat_ears`, `hw_pumpkin_shoes`).
  - Added visual badges (`[Сет]`, `[Комната]`) to items in the probability editor grid.
  - Implemented automatic pool healing/reconciliation in `switchOddsMode("case_hw")` that replaces stale 4-item prototypes in cloud/localStorage with the complete 13-item pool without resetting valid chances.
  - Added `slot_wish` (💌 Купон на желание, 1%) to Slots 777 odds table.
- **Mobile Viewport & Styling Resilience** (`index.html`, `dashboard.html`, `app.js`, `css/tailwind.css`):
  - Pre-compiled 77 KB standalone CSS bundle (`css/tailwind.css`) to eliminate reliance on external JIT Tailwind CDN on mobile data networks.
  - Inlined anti-FOUC and layout lock rules in `<style>` to ensure elements never collapse into normal block flow or display unstyled text.
  - Guarded `petRoomCard` layout classes (`w-full bg-[#141527]/90 shadow-xl`) in `applyRoomTheme`.

  - Rich actionable cards in «Ящик предложений и любовных записок» with instant status tags (`📋 Канбан`, `💖 Похвала принята`).
  - One-click «📋 В Канбан» transfer button with target task category selector.

  - Fixed issue where client cloud sync wiped out events generated by dashboard or vice-versa.

- **AI Economy Advisor & Critical Risk Harmonization** (`admin_modules/admin_economy.js`):
  - Resolved 55% Critical Risk discrepancy where the risk badge was red while recommendations were blank.
  - Every risk factor now generates actionable recommendations or detailed diagnostic cards, even when bets/costs hit threshold caps.

## [2.7.0] - 2026-10-16

### Added
- **Lex Labs Achievements & Hall of Fame Engine** (`game_modules/achievements_db.js`, `index.html`, `app.js`):
  - 50 full achievements across 7 thematic categories: Pet Care (`pet`), Wardrobe & Atelier (`wardrobe`), Lootboxes (`cases`), Casino Hub (`casino`), Arcade Skill (`arcade`), Romance & Wishes (`love`), and Hidden Easter Eggs (`secret`).
  - Tiered ranking system with Gamerscore (Мур-Очки: Bronze 10, Silver 25, Gold 50, Mythic 100) totaling 1,665 points and 5 progression ranks.
  - Interactive «Зал Славы» modal (`modalAchievements`) featuring category filters, progress bar, rank badge, and dynamic achievement cards.
  - Linear/Vercel-style floating achievement toast notifications with glowing tier borders, Web Audio sound effects (`sound.epicWin()`), particle confetti, and haptic feedback.
  - Persistent state management with `katya_master_achievements` in localStorage and automatic synchronization with cloud data.
  - Comprehensive hooks connected across all minigames, feeding, sleeping, poop cleaning, wardrobe fitting, casino slots/crash, and support chat.

### Added
- **Star Sparks Duplicate Protection & Atelier Exchange System** (`game_modules/items_db.js`, `game_modules/minigames/cases.js`):
  - Dynamic duplicate reward calculation based on item rarity (`common`, `rare`, `epic`, `halloween`, `legendary`, `mythic`) and drop chance percentage ($\le 10\%$, $\le 5\%$).
  - Full bundle breakdown logic: individual evaluation of elements in multi-item sets, granting new items and accumulating Star Sparks for duplicates.
  - Interactive «✨ Ателье» tab embedded in the Wardrobe modal with 3 exchange recipes: coins, guaranteed missing outfit (pity protection), and secret wish coupon.
  - Royal pet treat (+40% hunger, +30% happy) and romantic compliment generator from Alexey upon rolling duplicate items.
  - Live spark balance badges integrated into App Header, Wardrobe Modal, and Casino Hub.
- **Macroeconomy Balance & AI Advisor Integration** (`admin_modules/admin_economy.js`):
  - Expected duplicate value ($1 \text{ ✨} \approx 20 \text{ 🪙}$) factored into case RTP equations, ensuring stable 75–82% return even with collected wardrobes.
  - Comprehensive Halloween Crypt RTP audit added to LiveOps engine.
  - AI Economy Advisor monitors player Star Sparks stockpile ($\ge 20 \text{ ✨}$) and suggests Atelier promotions.
- **Support Desk (Tier-1 CRM Decision Tree) Enhancements** (`admin_modules/admin_main.js`, `dashboard.html`):
  - Added «✨ Звёздные Искры и Повторки» node under the Drops branch with 1-click spark compensation (+2, +6, +10 ✨) and 1C Ledger audit trails.
  - Added `editPetSparks` input in Mission Control dashboard.
  - Added canned response templates for duplicate compensations and Atelier invitations.
